# Local SEO reference

## Local ranking factors (Google's own framing)

Google states local results are based on **relevance**, **distance**, and **prominence**.

- Relevance: GBP categories, services, description, website content matching the search.
- Distance: from the searcher (or the location in the query) to the business. Cannot be optimized, only respected (target areas the business can win).
- Prominence: reviews (count, rating, recency), links and mentions, citations, general web presence.

## GBP rules that cause suspensions

- Keywords or locations stuffed into the business name.
- Address at a virtual office, mailbox (UPS Store), or coworking space without staffed presence.
- Multiple listings for one business at one location, or listings for each service area.
- Review gating, incentivized reviews, reviews written by the owner/staff/agency.

## Service-area business (SAB) notes

- Hide the address if customers are not served at that location.
- Service areas: up to 20 areas; keep within roughly a 2-hour drive.
- SABs rank strongest near their verified address; distant towns need strong website relevance and prominence, and still may not show in the map pack.

## Location page quality checklist

A page for `<Service> in <Town>` earns its place if it has most of:

- Projects completed in that town (photos, short write-up).
- Testimonials from customers there.
- Specific local information: permits/codes, housing stock, climate, typical issues, travel time, local partners.
- Staff or crew connected to the area.
- Unique FAQs.
- Embedded map or directions if there is a physical location.

If it has none: do not build it. Mention the town on the service page or service-area hub instead.

## Citation priority (US)

1. Google Business Profile, Bing Places, Apple Business Connect.
2. Facebook, Yelp, BBB, Nextdoor.
3. Data aggregators (Data Axle, Foursquare, Neustar Localeze).
4. Industry: Angi, HomeAdvisor, Houzz, Thumbtack, Porch (home services); Avvo, Justia (legal); Healthgrades, Zocdoc (medical).
5. Local: chamber of commerce, local business associations, local news business directories.

## Example market: York, Pennsylvania

Common service areas around York: York, West York, North York, York Township, Spring Garden Township, Springettsbury, Manchester, Dover, Red Lion, Dallastown, Shrewsbury, Hanover, Wrightsville, Hellam. Neighboring markets: Lancaster, Harrisburg, Gettysburg, northern Maryland. Use only the towns the client actually serves.

The skill is geography-agnostic: replace with the client's real market.

## Review response template ideas

- Positive: thank by name, mention the specific project/service and town naturally, invite them back.
- Negative: acknowledge, apologize for the experience (not admitting fault in legal matters), move offline with a contact name and number. Never reveal private details.
