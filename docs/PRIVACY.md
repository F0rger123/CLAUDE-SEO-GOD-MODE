# Privacy policy: SEO God Mode browser extension

_Last updated: 2026-10-26_

SEO God Mode ("the extension") checks web pages for SEO, AEO and AI-search readiness. It's published by Agency Zero (https://theagencyzero.com) as part of the open-source CLAUDE SEO GOD MODE project.

## What the extension accesses

When you press **Scan this page**, the extension reads the page you're viewing and requests these files from that same website: `robots.txt`, the XML sitemap, `llms.txt`, one made-up URL (to check that missing pages return 404), and the site's `http://` address. It only does this for sites you choose to scan, after you grant access to that site.

## What it stores

Scan results (scores, check results and the page facts behind them) are saved in your browser's local extension storage on your device, so you can compare before and after. It keeps up to 25 scans per page. You can remove them at any time by removing the extension or clearing its data.

## What it shares

Nothing. The extension has no servers, no analytics, no advertising and no accounts. It doesn't sell, transfer or share any data with anyone. Prompts and reports leave your device only when you copy or download them yourself.
